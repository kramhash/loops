# SPINE

## Title
SPINE — a column that turns on the kick

## Description
Hexagonal layers stacked on a dark spine, their rims the only light. Each bar of the drop turns them a new way: opposing, in a wave, springing apart.
暗い軸に積まれた六角形の層。drop の小節ごとに回り方が変わります（逆回転・波・開き）。

Raymarched GLSL with frapx, sequenced with vixeq. Code: github.com/kramhash/loops
