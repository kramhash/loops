# NUCLEUS

## Title
NUCLEUS — a living mass that splits on the kick

## Description
Five fused lobes, lit only along their rim. It breathes through the calm; in the drop every kick splits it apart (four ways, a spinning ring, two halves) and it fuses again before the next beat.
縁の光だけで照らされた塊。drop ではキックごとに裂けて、次の拍までに融合します。

Raymarched GLSL with frapx, sequenced with vixeq. Code: github.com/kramhash/loops
