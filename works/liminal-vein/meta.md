# LIMINAL VEIN

## Title
LIMINAL VEIN — a tunnel that breathes with its song

## Description
A seamless 15-second loop: a quiet tunnel lit only by its rings. In the drop every kick throws the camera forward and turns the tunnel from circle to hexagon to star.
曲に合わせて呼吸するトンネル。drop ではキックごとに断面が円→六角→星形に変わります。

Raymarched GLSL with frapx, sequenced with vixeq. Code: github.com/kramhash/loops
